About

I'm a PhD researcher in Informatics at the University of Iowa, with expertise in multimodal machine learning, large language models, and clinical AI applications. My work focuses on developing and rigorously evaluating AI systems for biomedical text processing, clinical decision support, and healthcare informatics.

I combine computational research with domain expertise in medical informatics to address real-world challenges in digital health and biomedical research. I'm passionate about creating AI solutions that are both technically sophisticated and clinically meaningful.

Projects

Recent research initiatives and technical projects.

2024 — Present
DMP Bridge: Data Management Plan Extraction Pipeline
An open-source Python framework for extracting Data Management Plan fields from PDF documents using advanced NLP and LLM-based classification. Supports RDA Common Standard JSON conversion with automated pattern matching and neural classification.
Python LLMs NLP PDF Processing
2023 — 2024
Comparative Evaluation of AI-Generated Data Management Plans
Comprehensive evaluation of GPT-4, Llama 3.3, and human-written DMPs using automated metrics (SBERT, ROUGE) and expert human assessment. Presented at ISMB-BOSC 2026 as part of the Zeinali et al. research series.
GPT-4 Llama Evaluation Research
2022 — 2023
Clinical NLP for Adverse Event Detection
Developed multimodal machine learning models for detecting and classifying adverse events from clinical narratives. Achieved 94% F1-score using ensemble methods combining BERT-based text processing with structured data features.
BERT NLP Machine Learning Clinical Data
